# Practice rules

Each player starts at Launch with 1,500 simulated XLM. You play first, followed by Orbit. Dice are two independent values from one to six; no difficulty level changes dice outcomes.

1. Roll, resolve the landing, optionally buy, then end your turn.
2. Passing or landing on Launch earns 200 XLM once per lap. Other corners do not award cash.
3. Unowned properties cost the listed price. You may buy only your current offered property and must have sufficient cash. Skipping does not charge you.
4. Landing on another player's property transfers the listed rent. Owning every property of its color doubles that group's rent, including the gray and pale utility groups. There are no houses, mortgages, auctions, or property resale in this edition.
5. Chance and community spaces draw from separate four-card decks, with replacement. Cards add or subtract cash immediately.
6. Network Upkeep costs 200 XLM; Rug Pull costs 100 XLM. These are fictional game fees.
7. Take a Break moves the player directly to Waiting Room without a Launch bonus. Their next roll is skipped and detention ends. Landing naturally on Waiting Room is only a visit.
8. Orbit buys when the purchase leaves at least 500 XLM on Casual, 250 on Strategic, or 100 on Competitive.
9. After thirty player-and-bot rounds, cash plus property purchase values decide the winner. Equal totals draw.
10. A mandatory payment greater than available cash ends the match immediately. An opponent receiving rent gets only the available cash. Paying exactly the remaining cash is allowed.

The browser saves each accepted action. If the page is closed during Orbit's turn, returning to the game resumes that turn. Leaving for another app view pauses an outstanding bot timer until the game room is opened again.

Practice balances have no monetary value. Connecting a wallet has no effect on these rules.
